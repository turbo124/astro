Mollie is an online payment platform designed for businesses and freelancers in the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland, and the United Kingdom, serving over 130,000 businesses.

## Payment Methods

- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
- Major European payment platforms (Cartes Bancaires, Giropay, and others)
- Direct bank transfers for participating banks
- Global credit card payments despite EU focus

## Recurring Payments

Set up auto-billing and recurring payments on invoices from your most loyal clients and customers.
