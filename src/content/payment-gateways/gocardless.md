GoCardless is a direct debit payment solution that automates invoice collection. As soon as your invoice is due, GoCardless automatically collects payment, so you don't need to chase.

## Key Benefits

- **Stress Reduction**: Automatic payment collection on due dates
- **Administrative Efficiency**: Spend 59% less time managing payments through automated reconciliation
- **Customer Preference**: 78% of businesses globally prefer account-to-account payment methods

## How It Works

1. Customers enter payment details securely and authorize direct debit collection
2. You invoice normally through Invoice Ninja
3. Payment is automatically collected on the due date and reconciled
