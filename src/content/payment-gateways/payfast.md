Payfast is a South African-based digital payment solution. It requires a South African bank account (payments received in ZAR), but customers can be located anywhere globally.

## Payment Methods

- Credit cards (Visa and Mastercard, covering ~80% of the credit card market)
- Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) with typically lower fees
- QR code payments
- Automatic recurring billing for subscriptions and retainer-based work
