Invoice Ninja offers Stripe Connect as a recommended payment gateway solution. The platform enables freelancers and small-to-medium businesses to accept online payments securely and improve cash flow management.

## Payment Methods Supported

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Bank transfers via ACH
- Digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- Regional options: SEPA Direct Debit, iDEAL, Bancontact, EPS, SOFORT

## Setup

1. Create or log into an Invoice Ninja account
2. Navigate to Settings > Payment Settings > Add Payment Gateway
3. Select and authenticate with Stripe Connect

## Geographic Availability

Stripe operates in 47+ countries including Canada, Brazil, Australia, and throughout Europe.

## Fund Deposits

Initial deposits require a minimum seven-day holding period for compliance purposes. Subsequent transfers typically arrive within 2-3 business days in the US and Australia, or up to seven business days in Canada, UK, and Ireland.
