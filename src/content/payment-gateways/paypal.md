Invoice Ninja integrates with PayPal and Venmo to enable freelancers and small business owners to receive payments from clients worldwide.

## Key Features

- Access to over 300 million PayPal customers globally
- Support for 25+ currencies
- Available in numerous countries including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and European nations
- Customers can pay via PayPal balance, PayPal Credit, bank accounts, debit/credit cards, or Venmo

## Setup

1. Create or log into your Invoice Ninja account
2. Navigate to Settings > Payment Settings > New Gateway
3. Select "PayPal Platform" and authenticate with your PayPal account
