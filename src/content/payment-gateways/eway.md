eWAY is a global omnichannel payment platform operated by Global Payments Inc., enabling businesses to process secure credit card payments.

## Key Benefits

- **Simplified Billing**: Automates invoice creation and delivery
- **Transparent Pricing**: Flat-rate structure with no hidden charges
- **Fast Settlement**: Quick payment clearing to protect cash flow
- **24/7 Support**: Dedicated technical support team
