Braintree is a PayPal-affiliated payment platform that integrates seamlessly with Invoice Ninja.

## Key Features

- Credit card payments without clients needing additional accounts
- Digital wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay
- Multi-currency support: 130+ currencies
- Automatic and recurring billing for regular monthly orders

Integration is a snap, with flexible APIs that fit easily in your website. By partnering with Invoice Ninja, you're only an API away from linking Braintree to your account.
