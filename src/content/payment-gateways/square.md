Square is a payment processing platform that integrates with Invoice Ninja for seamless online payment processing.

## Key Features

- Accepts all major credit cards with consistent flat-rate fees
- Supports recurring monthly charges and subscription services
- Automatic payment notifications and preset reminder emails
- Automatic conversion of quotes into invoices
- Quick setup in just a few clicks
