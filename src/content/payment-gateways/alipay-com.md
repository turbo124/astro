Alipay.com is a global digital payment platform serving over 700 million active users worldwide, processing transactions in more than 14 foreign currencies.

## Key Features

- **Security**: Advanced encryption technology protects transactions
- **QR Code Payments**: Contact-free payment options
- **Escrow Services**: Verification before payment release
- **Global Accessibility**: Multi-language support for international clients
- **300M+ users** in China specifically
