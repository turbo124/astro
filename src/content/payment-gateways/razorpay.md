Razorpay enables businesses to accept payments through over 100 payment methods with setup taking less than five minutes and no coding required.

## Key Features

- **Payment Links**: Share invoices via links through email, messenger, or SMS
- **Smart Collect**: Automates reconciliation with support for RTGS, UPI, IMPS, and NEFT
- **Payment Splitting**: Divide incoming payments to specific accounts using APIs

## Setup

1. Navigate to Settings > Online Payments
2. Click "Configure Gateways"
3. Select Razorpay from available providers
4. Complete authentication in under five minutes
