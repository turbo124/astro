BTCPay Server is a self-hosted, open-source cryptocurrency payment processor that emphasizes security, privacy, and decentralization without transaction fees.

## Key Benefits

- **Zero fees** on cryptocurrency transactions
- **Self-hosted** infrastructure for complete control
- **Open-source** technology ensuring transparency
- **Censorship-resistant** payment processing
- **No third-party** intermediaries required

## Setup

1. Create or access your Invoice Ninja account
2. Navigate to Settings > Payment Settings > Add Payment Gateway
3. Select "BTCPay" from available providers
