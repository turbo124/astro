Apple Pay has become the most popular way to make online payments, replacing physical cards and cash. Invoice Ninja integrates Apple Pay to let your clients pay invoices instantly from their Apple devices.

## Security & Privacy

Device-specific numbers and transaction codes are used to maintain privacy. Credit or debit card numbers aren't stored on devices or Apple servers, and customer card numbers won't be shared with you.

## How It Works

1. Payment request displays on their iPhone
2. One-touch approval using stored payment method
3. Transaction encrypted with device-specific codes
4. Invoice automatically marked as paid in your account
