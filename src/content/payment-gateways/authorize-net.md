Authorize.net operates as a Visa Inc. subsidiary specializing in payment processing and management services.

## Setup

1. Create a merchant account on Authorize.net's website
2. Access your Invoice Ninja account and navigate to Settings > Payment Settings
3. Select "New Gateway" and choose Authorize.net
4. Enter your API Login ID and Transaction Key from your Authorize.net merchant account

Authorize.net enables you to process payments in any way that works for your business, whether it's through your Invoice Ninja account or manual transactions.
