### Our updated PayPal integration now includes Venmo!

This PayPal update is ready inside your Invoice Ninja account!

It’s free and easy to update your PayPal integration. It comes with Venmo and an improved checkout for your customers.

### Venmo users spend 2x's more.

Our new PayPal integration now offers Venmo for your customers! And it's FREE!

### How to update with Venmo?

- **[Log in](https://app.invoicing.co/#/login) to your Invoice Ninja account. **

- **Under account settings: [Settings>Payment Settings, "New Gate](https://app.invoicing.co/#/settings/online_payments)[way"](https://app.invoicing.co/#/settings/online_payments)  add the new 'PayPal Platform' integration!**

*If you have an existing 'PayPal Express' integration, first click to delete 'PayPal Express' and then click "New Gateway" to add 'PayPal Platform.' That's it!

### Now you're ready to get paid!
