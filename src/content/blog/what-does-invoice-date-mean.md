## Invoices and Their Role

An invoice is an itemized list of the products or services delivered by a business or an individual to their customers. The information typically features the total amount that is due and a method for the client to pay for the items or services in question. Invoices can be sent through electronic means or paper.

Invoices can be paid either through one-time payments or in installments, and users can even schedule multiple payments for a single invoice if agreed to by both parties.

## The Upside of Online Invoicing

There are numerous benefits to sending online electronic invoices compared to their paper counterparts. For example, they result in quicker payments. This also applies if you want to [send a paid invoice](https://invoiceninja.com/how-to-send-a-paid-invoice/) after receiving the funds.

This results in improved cash flow, and faster payments can ensure that businesses and service providers are more likely to remain in the green. Additionally, fewer materials are used which saves on costs associated with printing paper or purchasing printing equipment. Electronic invoices are also far easier to track.

Physical invoices can pile up or get lost in cabinets. However, when sent digitally, anyone can search for them in their Inbox or on the local computer where they were initially stored.

## The Invoice Date Explained

You may be curious [why invoices generally include an invoice number](https://invoiceninja.com/why-do-invoices-generally-include-an-invoice-number/). Each invoice will have numerous essential details and play a major role in its overall functionality and legitimacy. One such type of information is the invoice date. 

The invoice date is the date of the issuing of the document. However, it does not represent the date the products or services were provided.

The invoice date reflects when the person or company that delivered the goods or services generated the invoice and requested payment. The receipt date is when the services were received or contractually due to be completed.

Each invoice needs dates that clarify the obligations the issuer and recipient have.

The payment terms correspond to the invoice date. The date is a way to record when individual invoices were issued and allow customers and clients to distinguish one invoice from another.

The invoice date should be displayed in an easy-to-read way to prevent any confusion.

Typically, this is completed through the usage of a sequential numbering system, one that ensures that they are easily produced, paid, and tracked even if there are numerous invoices generated with the same invoice date.
