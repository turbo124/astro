Since our launch in 2014, community members like yourself have helped us to build the best suite of apps for small businesses.

At Invoice Ninja, our mission has always been to deliver the best possible value to our incredible community of users, and that includes you! Your unwavering support and feedback have propelled us forward over the past decade. We haven't updated pricing in well over 4+ years, however in response to rising input costs across the board, we are implementing a modest adjustment to our pricing which will go into effect January 1, 2024:

### Pro Plans will adjust from $10/ month to $12/ month.

#### Enterprise Plans will adjust per User # tiers:

▪ 1-2 Account Users: $16/ month

▪ 3-5 Account Users: $28/ month

▪ 6-10 Account Users: $40/ month

▪ 11-20 Account Users: $48/ month

▪ 21-30 Account Users: $64/ month

*With every annual plan you pay only 10 months + receive 2 months free!
