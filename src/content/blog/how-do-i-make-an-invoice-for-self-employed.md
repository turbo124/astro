There are several ways to [write your first invoice](https://invoiceninja.com/how-do-i-write-my-first-invoice/), but a user-friendly, [simple freelance invoice template](https://invoiceninja.com/invoice-templates-3/gig-freelancer-invoice-template/) is often the best solution if you’re new to running a self-employed business.

Invoices need to include several mandatory pieces of information to be valid and payable. The formatting and quality of your invoice documentation can make a huge difference to the speed of payments and the professional impression you make.

## Ways to Create Invoices as a Self-Employed Freelancer

Freelancers have three primary options when it comes to creating invoices, but whichever route you pick, it’s essential that you use a consistent [freelancer invoice format](https://invoiceninja.com/freelancer-invoice-format/) on each document:

- Manual invoicing: Paper-based or hand-typed invoices aren't advisable since the potential to make mistakes, miss something out, or forget to include a chargeable service is high.

- Freelance portal invoices: If you work through a freelance platform, you might be able to create invoices within your workflow. However, creating invoices separately is wise since most portals don’t allow you to download comprehensive accounting records, and your payment values will be reduced by the commission charged.

- Invoice templates: This is a great opportunity to utilize free, professional, and correctly laid out invoices, where you can work through prompts to populate every field.

A standardized and consistent freelancer invoice format is ideal since you can follow the same layout for every document, featuring your branding or logo and payment details. Your invoices reflect your attention to detail and can affect your credibility. We recommend using a template to ensure each document is valid, clear and doesn't give rise to queries.

Invoices with missing details or that appear incomplete will mean your payments are likely delayed, and you'll spend more time managing your bookkeeping admin chasing late remittances or re-issuing corrected invoices.
