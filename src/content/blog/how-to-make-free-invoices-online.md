## What You Need to Create a Free Invoice

To [make a professional-looking invoice](https://invoiceninja.com/how-to-make-a-professional-looking-invoice/) for your business, you’ll need the following:

### 1. Information about Your Business and the Customer

Assemble necessary information, including your contact details, the customer's contact details, the date of the invoice, and the detailed description of services and/or products purchased or provided. You'll make it easier for your customer to pay if you provide detailed information.

### 2. Payment Terms

For each invoice, clearly state what payment terms apply such as due dates, payment methods accepted (e.g., credit card or PayPal), late payment fees (if applicable), etc. This way, you'll avoid any delays or misunderstandings when it comes to making payments.

### 3. Accurate Calculations

To ensure that all amounts billed are accurate and include any applicable taxes or discounts, carefully review your invoices before sending them out. This way, you know that you’re charging the correct amount and the customer isn’t over or undercharged.

### 4. Templates

Creating an invoice from scratch every time you need one isn't practical. This is why Invoice Ninja invoice software includes templates you can customize to fit your business. With pre-made templates, you can easily create a professional-looking invoice in no time.

### 5. Signature

Depending on the country in which you do business, you may need to include an official signature on each invoice before it can be considered legally binding and valid.
