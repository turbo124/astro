## How to Create an Invoice for Hourly Services

To create an invoice for your hourly services, you have to compute the hourly pay rate for each task and all job descriptions that you complete within the hour. This invoice consolidates tasks done per hour and specifies the obligation of payment.

Many freelance specialists and contractors bill their clients on an hourly basis because they’re paid hourly rates. So, as a freelancer, you have to keep a record of the hours you’ve worked for each client and invoice them at the end of each project cycle. Your hourly invoice should indicate what you’re billing for, the amount of time you’ve spent on each task, as well as the hourly rate for each task.

Your invoice for hourly services should contain other important details, like your name and contact details, as well as those of your client. It should also contain:

- An invoice number

- Date of issuance

- Due date

- Amount in figures

- The subtotal

- The grand total

- Tax obligations and other fees

At the bottom of your invoice, include a footer, which includes essential details like payment instructions (mode of payment and possible fines for late payments) and the expected payment timelines. If you’re using an invoice template, choose the one that provides all these fields so that you don’t leave essential details out.

Once you’ve filled in all the invoice details, send it to your client via email or through the invoicing platform. You can ask your client what their preferred communication channel is to avoid delays.
