### What is auto-billing for recurring invoicing?

If you have clients that need to be billed on a regular basis (daily, weekly, monthly, etc…), then you know exactly how time consuming this can be! First, you have to keep their credit card or bank details on file. Next, you must remember the date of the upcoming invoice. And then, you have to manually invoice the client. Every single time. All this on top of your regular work schedule and invoicing tasks.

If this spells 'hassle' to you, Invoice Ninja comes to the rescue with token billing support via the world's favorite payment gateways:

- Square
- Eway Rapid
- PayFast
- Checkout.com
- Paytrace
- Forte

### What is token billing?

Token billing is a feature that enables the payments system to capture your clients' credit card details and store them for billing at a future date. In short, token billing is what allows auto-billing, which means future recurring invoice payments can be processed automatically.

### Why would I need to auto-bill?

Perhaps you handle gigs on a regular basis for a particular client. It could be daily, weekly, monthly, or another frequency. Or maybe you have subscribers, who you need to bill every month, every six months… or even annually.

Whatever the frequency and whatever the reason, token billing via Invoice Ninja provides the ultimate auto-billing solution for fixed recurring payments. Simply define the recurring invoice details, sit back, and let the token billing feature do the hard work for you.
