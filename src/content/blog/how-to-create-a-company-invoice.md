## Creating Invoices for Your Customers

To [make a professional-looking invoice](https://invoiceninja.com/how-to-make-a-professional-looking-invoice/), follow these eight simple steps:

### 1. Create Brand Identifiers

Brand identifiers are a great way to make your invoice stand out from the competition, and a solid brand will help you establish credibility and trust with clients. Customers will easily recognize and associate your invoice with your company–identifiers can include logos, headers, taglines, and even the company name and address.

### 2. Gather Invoice Details

Before you start creating your invoice, make sure to have all the necessary information. This includes the customer’s name and address, a unique invoice number, a list of items purchased or services rendered, the amount due, any applicable taxes and fees, and payment terms. This way, you’ll be able to accurately create and send your invoice without any mistakes.

### 3. Select an Invoice Template

After you have all the details, it’s time to choose a template. Invoicing software like Invoice Ninja offers free business invoice templates so you can quickly create your invoice without having to design it from scratch. By tweaking the template to match your brand, you can make sure that your invoice looks professional and catches the attention of your customers.

### 4. Choose the Right Formatting

When creating an invoice, it’s important to pay attention to the formatting. Font size, spacing between lines and items, and alignment are all factors that can affect how your invoice looks. Select a font that’s easy to read, and keep the layout consistent throughout the document.

### 5. Include Relevant Company Details

Now, it’s time to fill in the details. Start by including your company’s contact information, such as its name, address, and phone number. Then, fill in the customer’s details like their name and address. Include the invoice number, a list of items purchased or services rendered, and the amount due. 

### 6. Specify Payment Requirements and Terms of Service

Once you have all the details spelled out, specify the payment requirements and terms of service–this includes any applicable taxes and fees as well as when they are due. You can also add a note about late payment fees or a discount for early payment. For example, you may charge an extra 5% if the payment is late. This way, customers will be more likely to pay on time.

### 7. Check for Accuracy, Grammar, and Spelling Mistakes

Before you send out the invoice, double-check that all of the information on the invoice is accurate and that there are no grammar or spelling mistakes in the document. This is especially important when dealing with invoices, as mistakes may reflect poorly on your business.

### 8. Send the Invoice and Follow Up

Now that you have a professional invoice ready to send, it’s time to do just that. You can email the invoice or print it out and mail it. If you send it via email, make sure to follow up with a reminder to confirm whether the customer received it. It’s also good practice to send payment reminders if the customer hasn’t paid on time.
