To make things even more difficult, many invoicing software options come with a high price tag, putting a strain on small businesses and freelancers.

## Key Features

When it comes to finding the best free invoice software, there are a few key features to look for.

Easy UI: Firstly, the software should be easy to use and navigate, allowing you to create invoices quickly and efficiently. It should also be customizable, allowing you to add your own branding and logos to your invoices.

Additionally, the software should be able to track invoices, so you can see which ones have been paid and which are still outstanding.

Automation: Another important feature of invoice software is the ability to automate payments. This means that when you send an invoice, the recipient can easily pay directly from the invoice without having to transfer funds manually. This saves time, reduces the chance of human error, and makes it easier to keep track of payments.

Integration: Another important feature of any [online invoice system](https://invoiceninja.com/what-is-an-online-invoice-system/) is the ability to integrate with other tools and platforms you already use. This helps shorten your invoicing process, making it easier and more efficient. For example, if you use a CRM, your invoice software should be able to integrate with it, so you can easily see which invoices have been sent to which clients.

Security: It's important to consider the security and privacy of your invoicing software. The best invoice software should have strong security measures in place to protect your financial data and comply with privacy regulations.

Recurring Invoices: This is particularly useful for businesses that bill clients regularly, such as subscription-based services. With this feature, the software will automatically generate and send invoices at a specified interval, reducing the need for manual invoice creation.
