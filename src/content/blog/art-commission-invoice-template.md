## How Art Commissions Work

Art commissions are specialized requests from a client or a group of clients tasking an artist to create new pieces of artwork specifically tailored to their exclusive needs. 

In other words, when someone wants to commission an artist, the buyer contacts the artist and hires them directly to create a new piece that the client will pay for when it is received. In other words, a commission is a request from the buyer for the artist to create an artwork according to the specific parameters and themes a buyer wants. 

Some commissions might entail redoing a painting in a different style or size that the client wants, or creating new artwork that can be used for a specific business in the form of a card, banner, billboard, or anything else. The unique style of an artist is their selling point.

### Start by Promoting

The artist, in this case, needs to let the world know they are available for work and that they do indeed accept commission requests. They can do this through a page or statement on their social media channels.

Typically, artists will specify that they are experts at certain types of art, what they genuinely like doing, and what type of work they will accept a commission for.

The client will then reach out to the artist. They will set up a virtual meeting or an in-person get-together if they are nearby to each other. 

### How Invoice Templates Can Help

Before the project begins, the artist and the client will define the commission work, payment schedule and methods, and the rights to the artwork. The agreement is memorialized with their signatures. 

When the work is finished or at previously agreed upon intervals, an art commission invoice will be sent by the artist as a way to request payments for the work. These details will typically be included in an initial contract.

The artist may collect a partial payment upfront and the balance after delivering the finalized product.
