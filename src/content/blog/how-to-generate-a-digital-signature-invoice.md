## The Benefits of Digital Signatures

With the commonality of digital transactions and e-commerce, the need for secure and convenient methods of electronic invoicing has never been greater. Digital signatures play a crucial role in ensuring the authenticity and integrity of electronic invoices when you [generate invoices online](https://invoiceninja.com/how-to-generate-invoices-online/).

There’s always a risk of tampering, forgery, or fraud with traditional invoices; however, digital signatures use advanced encryption algorithms to secure the invoice, ensuring it cannot be altered or tampered with. This provides a much higher level of protection for both the sender and the recipient of the invoice.

Traditional invoices can be prone to errors, such as misplacing signatures or missing information. Digital signatures ensure all necessary information is included and all parties involved are aware of the terms and conditions of the invoice, which improves the accuracy and reliability of invoices and reduces the risk of disputes or misunderstandings.

With traditional invoices, the process of signing, printing, and sending can be time-consuming and labor-intensive. Digital signatures eliminate these steps, allowing for invoices to be created, signed, and sent in a matter of minutes. This saves businesses time and resources.

Digital signatures eliminate the need for paper, reducing businesses’ carbon footprint and saving resources. On top of that, the cost of paper, ink, and postage associated with traditional invoicing methods can be substantial—digital signatures are much more cost-effective.

## Creating Digital Signatures

Generating digital signature invoices with Invoice Ninja is easy.

### Step 1: Create an Invoice in Invoice Ninja

Start by logging into your Invoice Ninja account and creating a new invoice. Enter all necessary information, such as the client's details, the items or services being invoiced, and the total amount.

### Step 2: Add the Digital Signature Field

Once the invoice is complete, under the ‘Authorizations' tab, click on the ‘Advanced Settings’ button and select ‘Require client to provide their signature.’ This will add a signature field to the invoice where the client can sign.

### Step 3: Send the Invoice

After adding the signature field, you can send the invoice to the client via email or any other preferred method. The client will receive a link to the invoice, where they can review the details and sign it.

### Step 4: The Client Signs the Invoice

The client can sign the invoice by simply clicking on the signature field and using their mouse or touch screen to sign. Once the client signs the invoice, the signature will be securely stored in the Invoice Ninja database.

### Step 5: Download or Print the Signed Invoice

After the client signs the invoice, you can download or print a copy of the invoice for your records. The signed invoice will also be stored in your Invoice Ninja account for future reference.
