## Calculating Billable Hours for a Consulting Invoice

You may decide to charge per hour, day, or project, but having a billing policy established from the beginning of the consultation or included within your initial proposal is advisable.

Progression tracking should be simple, using a systematic record-keeping approach to bill all hours worked, including travel time or meetings with your client.

### Designing Your Custom Consultation Invoice

Invoices should have a header at the top of the page stating that the document is an invoice and featuring your business name and logo. Contact details are helpful should your client need to ask any questions, and adding a phone number and email provides flexibility if further communications are necessary.

Our invoice templates include a field for the client's details. You should verify that the information is accurate, including the point of contact you have been liaising with, the relevant department, and the organization name.

### Information to Include in Invoices for Consulting Work

Every invoice should feature the date you created the document; this is auto-populated if you create an invoice from a template. Dates are important since they may affect budget periods or tax returns.

A professional invoice must also show an identifying number or reference, which can be as simple as ‘01’ but can include alphabetical characters if this helps you keep a record of which invoice relates to which client.

### Itemizing a Consultation Invoice

The more detail, the better–although invoices don’t have to include lengthy descriptions of the work carried out, explaining what the charge is for can prevent confusion. Each line on your invoice should show what the cost is for, how many hours or days it applies to, and the rate you are charging.

For example, that might look like this:

- ‘Consultation meetings with xxx on xxx: Five hours at $50 per hour: $250’

- ‘Consultation days at xxx: Two days at $300 per day: $600’

These are basic illustrations, but they show the standard format a client would expect to see on a consultation invoice, split into columns for readability. Tables are the norm because setting out the information into columns makes it easier to scroll across and check the hours charged and rates are consistent with the original consultation agreement.

### Setting Payment Terms for Consulting Invoices

Payment terms should be displayed on each invoice, particularly if you have specific terms, such as immediate payment or payment due within seven days.

Invoices shouldn't contain any surprises, and if you can accept more than one payment method, it may help speed up your payment turnaround times.
