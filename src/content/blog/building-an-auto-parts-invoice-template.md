## What Is an Invoice Template, and How Can It Be Tailored to Your Auto Business?

Invoice templates are documents designed according to a client’s needs, serving as a starting point for creating invoices for goods or services rendered. An invoice template will save you a lot of time and ensure that invoices are carried out regularly and accurately–these templates will vary across clients and industries. The most common type of invoice is a purchase invoice. 

[What is a purchase invoice](https://invoiceninja.com/what-is-a-purchase-invoice/), exactly? These invoices will typically include all the necessary fields for an invoice, such as:

- The name and address of the seller and the buyer

- The date of the transaction

- Description of the goods or services provided

- The quantity of labor hours or products received

- Price totals and fees

Your invoice can then be customized to suit the specific needs of your business. This includes options to add additional line items to your invoice, or adjust branding imagery and other stylistic choices (logos, color schemes, and more).
