## Steps for Making a Freelance Invoice From Scratch

If you want to create your invoice from scratch, follow these six steps:

### 1. Create a Professional Header

Start your invoice with a professional header. This should include your name or the name of your business as well as your contact information, like your name, address, phone number, and email address.

By creating a professional-looking header on each invoice, businesses can project a sense of legitimacy and gain client confidence in their services. Invest some time in creating a meaningful header with your company logo.

### 2. Number and Date the Invoice

After creating a header, add an invoice number (in case you need to reference it later) and the date. This is important because it allows businesses to track their invoices and keep better records of payments.

You can create an invoice number in any format, such as a numerical sequence (e.g., 00001, 00002) or with letters and numbers (e.g., INV2020-01, INV2020-02).

### 3. Provide a Detailed Breakdown of the Work

In the body of your invoice, provide a detailed breakdown of the work you completed and how much you're charging. Include each item or service and its price (with any applicable taxes). Clients will appreciate the transparency, which may increase the likelihood that you’ll get paid quickly. You can create a table or list to make the breakdown easier to read.

### 4. Payment Method and Terms

At the bottom of your invoice, provide payment methods and terms. This should include what type of payments you accept, how customers can pay you (e.g., bank transfer, credit card, PayPal, etc.), when payments are due, and any late-payment penalties.

If you are using a SaaS invoicing platform like Invoice Ninja, you’ll have payment gateway integrations so clients can pay their invoices with one click. With over ten secure payment methods, your clients will be able to complete their payments quickly and safely.  

### 5. Include Your Details 

Include your name or the business name, address, email, and phone number at the end of the invoice. This helps customers who have any questions or concerns and provides a way for clients to follow up, if needed.
